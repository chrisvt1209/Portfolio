Iets meer over mij

I started in a creative environment and gradually moved deeper into development, where I found the balance between visual thinking and technical problem solving. That mix still shapes the way I approach every project.

I enjoy building interfaces that feel calm, clear and intentional while staying practical for editors and future developers. Whether I am working on a portfolio, a CMS-driven site or a reusable component library, I care about the details that make a product feel solid.

Outside the code itself, I like translating rough ideas into structured solutions and turning visual direction into something that is maintainable, responsive and pleasant to use.
